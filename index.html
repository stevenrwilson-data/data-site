About

How I work

My projects start with a question and use the methods that fit it.

I compare models when useful, test assumptions, examine where those assumptions break down, and explain what the results do — and do not — support. I’m especially interested in what happens when a model’s assumptions meet messy real-world data.

I have a BA in Mathematics from Cal State Fullerton and spent a summer at NASA using R for time-series analysis of instrument data across multiple missions. I work with R, SQL, and Python.
